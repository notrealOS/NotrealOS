# notreal.weather

Forecasts from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (no account, no key) in a Nothing-style look:
dot icons, a sun/moon disc and tiles, in every theme.

- Now, 48 hours, 10 days, a 2-hour rain nowcast, air quality and pollen
- Rain radar on the today page ([RainViewer](https://www.rainviewer.com))
- Swipe between saved places, or use GPS

Run: `./nrweather`
